# Hold-out sources

Every prior is trained on all twelve training sources (`hsr.grammar_compare.SOURCES`; `equal`: each source replicated to the size of the largest, `pooled`: every model-form pair once) and scored on the forms of each hold-out source, which no prior ever trains on. Mean log probability per model-form pair. raw: the prior's own log P (no renormalisation; tree priors share one space and sym0, the tree prior without context, is the honest baseline; PCFG grammars generate a different space, so raw scores compare only approximately across the two families); renorm: renormalised over a common support of 6684 forms (training, candidate and hold-out forms). Built by `snakemake --use-conda --cores 2 holdout`.
